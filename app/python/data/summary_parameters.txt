Critical interpretation:
- Runtime scheduler limits are now collected in app/python/ems/ems_limits.py.
- The price threshold creates two economic modes: low below the threshold and high at or above it.
- Demand is treated as a setpoint and compared directly with source discharge limits.
- Keep demand in mW in the app configuration.
- The battery is energy-scaled to 100 mWh for the EMS demo.
- PEM cutoff and full-test hydrogen end reading are not the same endpoint.
- PEM max usable power is below the absolute polarization-curve peak because voltage must stay above cutoff.
- PEM charging is treated as an energy accumulation problem, not as a fixed charging-current demand.
- One useful PEM output event is defined as 60 s at the selected usable PEM discharge power.
- That useful PEM output event requires about 12.8 J of PEM input charge energy, derived from the measured hydrogen production and consumption rates.
- PV can supply the 100 mW scaled load and can accumulate the derived useful PEM charge energy in the measured ramp tests.
